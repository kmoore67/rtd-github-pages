############################
Actuators
############################

Most of the actuators that are used for our competition robot are supplied by `Cross The Road Electronics CTRE <https://docs.ctre-phoenix.com/en/latest/index.html>`_.  These mainly include the **Kraken** for the drivetrain.  And the **TalonFX** for onboard mechanisms.  Before using any of this hardware we need to install the additional 3rd-Party *Phoenix* software.
